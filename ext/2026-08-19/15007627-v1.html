CuAAC-derived peptide amphiphiles P₂C₆, P₆C₆, P₈C₆, P₂C₈, P₆C₈, P₈C₈, P₂C₁₂, P₆C₁₂, and P₈C₁₂
HPLC reaction monitoring (1.0 mM azido-peptide, 1 eq. CuSO₄, 4 eq. ascorbic acid, 4 eq. alkyne) Each combination showed an immediate decrease in the azido-peptide HPLC peak within the first five minutes, accompanied by a new peak at a higher retention time, indicating that the newly formed species is more hydrophobic than its reactant; time-dependent analysis revealed monoexponential consumption of the starting material in all cases; equilibrium conversions of 35–60 %; all combinations reached equilibrium within approximately 20 minutes
DiO-C₆ fluorescence assembly monitoring C₆- and C₈-derived peptide amphiphiles rapidly assembled in contrast to their C₁₂-derived counterparts; the length of the alkyl spacer in the peptide precursor exerted negligible influence on the assembly rates; t₁/₂ ≈ 35 minutes for the shorter hexyne and octyne segments
Circular dichroism spectroscopy Almost all derivatives exhibited a characteristic random-coil signature; P₈C₁₂ showed β-sheet character
Confocal fluorescence microscopy after 5 h — C₆- and C₈-based peptide amphiphiles Isotropic colloidal morphology of micrometer-sized particles
Confocal fluorescence microscopy after 5 h — P₆C₁₂ and P₈C₁₂ Well-defined fibers with a strong tendency to form hierarchical structures via bundling
Confocal fluorescence microscopy — P₂C₁₂ Irregular architectures after 5 h; transitioned into fiber bundles matching those of P₆C₁₂ and P₈C₁₂ after 24 h
Confocal fluorescence microscopy after prolonged aging — P₂C₆ and P₂C₈ Fibrous morphology remained inaccessible

Fluorescence microscopy (EDC 75.0 mM; Ac-FRGRGD-OH 12.3 mM; pSS 4.3 kDa, 40.0 mM; control: fuel-dependent synthetic cells without peptide amphiphile assemblies) Early in the lifecycle, the droplets appeared almost identical to the control; the droplets continued to fuse, forming larger droplets; vacuolization resulted in spherical shells; irregular protrusions and thicker sections occurred in the shell structures; the observation was independent of peptide amphiphile molecular composition

Fuel-dependent synthetic cells with in situ P₂C₁₂ assembly
Fluorescence microscopy (7, 15, and > 20 min; NBD-G(RG)₂N-NH₂ and Cy5-labeled pSS derivative, 404 kDa) Within a few minutes, bright speckles appeared in the NBD-G(RG)₂N-NH₂ channel, indicating preferential accumulation of the peptide in a discrete domain within the coacervate; the same regions appeared as dark holes in the pSS channel, indicating exclusion of pSS; the domains matured into discrete assemblies resembling those formed by P₂C₁₂ after several hours of equilibration in isolation, indicating a secondary phase-separation of the in situ formed peptide amphiphile; pSS exclusion occurred before droplet vacuolization began; these discrete assemblies were never observed being expelled from the compartment altogether, nor fully exposed to either the vacuoles or the surrounding dilute phase

Passive coacervate droplets with P₂C₁₂ assembly
Fluorescence microscopy (phase separation with Ac-FRGRGN-NH₂) Structural transition from discrete to fibrous assemblies that can grow to lengths of up to several microns within about 1 h; fibrillar structures in isolation took upwards of 5 h to form; this structural transition preferentially occurs in larger droplets
