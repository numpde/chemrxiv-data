At 1 × 10⁻⁵ mbar O₂, the initial peak was of similar magnitude at all four temperatures; the steady-state CO₂ production rate increased with temperature; higher steady-state rates led to higher secondary peaks; and the second peak shifted to later times as temperature decreased.
