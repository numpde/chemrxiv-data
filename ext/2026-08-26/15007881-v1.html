CuSi₂P₃
Powder XRD, Rietveld refinement Sole crystalline product; space group: I4̅m2; a, b: 3.70(6) Å; c: 5.20(9) Å; α, β: 90°; γ: 90°; volume: 71.54(4) Å³; Rwp: 2.7 %; reduced χ²: 0.07; image data were not transcribed
TEM Polydisperse sample containing nanoparticles with sizes ranging from 50 to 200 nm; image data were not transcribed
HRTEM, SAED and STEM-HAADF Consistent with the orthorhombic unit cell of CuSi₂P₃; brighter Cu-rich and darker Si-rich atomic columns with a superstructure along the [010] direction were observed; no adjacent Cu columns were detected by STEM-HAADF; image data were not transcribed
EELS Homogeneous elemental distribution; the O-K signal was too low to be detected, even at the particle surface; image data were not transcribed
STEM-EDS and SEM-EDS Homogeneous distribution of Cu, Si and P at the scale of one particle; STEM-EDS Cu:Si:P atomic ratio: 1:1.9:2.6; SEM-EDS Cu:Si:P atomic ratio: 1:2.1:2.7; image data were not transcribed
Cu K-edge XANES and EELS Cu K-edge energy: 8980.1 eV, coincident with Cu₂O and indicating a copper oxidation state of +1; EELS average copper oxidation state: 1.3 ± 0.2; image data were not transcribed
Cu K-edge EXAFS Cu-P apparent distance: 1.9 Å; image data were not transcribed
Cu 2p XPS and Cu LMM Auger spectroscopy Cu 2p₃/₂ and Cu 2p₁/₂ peaks: 932.7 and 952.6 eV; no Cu²⁺ species were present at the particle surface; Cu⁺:Cu⁰ atomic ratio: 1:2; the apparent discrepancy with XAS was ascribed to partial reduction of Cu(I) in the XPS analysis chamber; image data were not transcribed
Si 2p XPS Si 2p₃/₂ and Si 2p₁/₂ peaks: 100.3 and 100.9 eV, ascribed to silicon covalently bonded to phosphorus as in [SiP₄] units; signals at 101.5 and 103.3 eV correspond to SiOₓ (x < 4) species ascribed to inadvertent air exposure; image data were not transcribed
P 2p XPS Broad phosphate-group signal at approximately 133.5 eV; P 2p₃/₂ and P 2p₁/₂ signals at 128.4 and 129.2 eV were assigned to a Cu-rich P environment; signals at 129.2 and 130.0 eV were assigned to a Si-rich P environment; image data were not transcribed
²⁹Si MAS NMR (139.04 MHz, 20 kHz MAS, referenced to TMS) δ = −26 ppm (single prominent peak); image data were not transcribed
³¹P MAS NMR (282.3 MHz, 20 kHz MAS, referenced to H₃PO₄) δ = −110, −150 ppm; image data were not transcribed
In situ Cu K-edge XANES (CO-saturated 1.0 M KOH, −0.83 V vs. RHE) Spectra were identical before and during electrocatalysis, suggesting stability of the catalytic copper species and no oxidation-state change; image data were not transcribed
In situ Cu K-edge EXAFS (CO-saturated 1.0 M KOH, −0.83 V vs. RHE) Constant apparent radial distance to near neighbours, consistent with Cu-P bonds at 1.87 Å in pristine CuSi₂P₃; Cu-Cu bonds expected for metallic Cu species were absent; image data were not transcribed
Post-catalysis TEM (CO reduction, −0.83 V vs. RHE) No alteration in particle shape, size or surface crystallinity was detected; image data were not transcribed
In situ and post-catalysis Cu Kβ valence-to-core X-ray emission spectroscopy (CO reduction, −0.83 V vs. RHE) No modification of the CuSi₂P₃ signal was observed; image data were not transcribed

Cu K-edge EXAFS Cu-S apparent distance: 1.9 Å; image data were not transcribed
Cu 2p XPS and Cu LMM Auger spectroscopy Cu 2p₃/₂ and Cu 2p₁/₂ peaks: 932.7 and 952.6 eV; Cu⁰:Cu⁺ atomic ratio: 1:39; Cu⁺ species: 96–98 at.%; image data were not transcribed
S 2p XPS S 2p₃/₂ and S 2p₁/₂ signals at 161.5 and 162.7 eV were assigned to S(−II) bonded to copper; signals at 162.4 and 163.6 eV were assigned to S(−I) bonded to phosphorus; sulfate groups were detected with a broad signal at 164 eV, indicating surface oxidation upon air exposure; image data were not transcribed
P 2p XPS No phosphate groups with oxidation state +5 were found; P 2p₃/₂ and P 2p₁/₂ peaks at 132.0 and 132.8 eV were assigned to phosphorus bonded to sulfur; image data were not transcribed
In situ Cu K-edge XANES (CO-saturated 1.0 M KOH, −0.83 V vs. RHE) Cu K-edge downshifted from 8980.7 to 8979.0 eV during electrocatalysis, indicating an average copper oxidation state of +0.2 and reduction of 80 % of the initial Cu⁺ species to Cu⁰; image data were not transcribed
In situ and post-catalysis Cu K-edge EXAFS (CO-saturated 1.0 M KOH, −0.83 V vs. RHE) Cu-S bonds at an apparent radial distance of 1.79 Å progressively disappeared in favour of Cu-Cu bonds at 2.16 Å; post-catalysis EXAFS indicated only Cu-O distances, mostly as Cu₂O; image data were not transcribed
Post-catalysis TEM and SAED (CO reduction, −0.83 V vs. RHE) Spherical Cu nanoparticles with diameters of 50–70 nm were surrounded by an amorphous layer consistent with a copper oxide layer; image data were not transcribed
