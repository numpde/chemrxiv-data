Single-crystal and polycrystalline LLZTO solid electrolytes
SEM and EBSD, well-polished surfaces Single-crystal LLZTO was largely featureless, whereas polycrystalline LLZTO exhibited a dense network of surface flaws, such as grain boundaries and voids intersecting the surface
XPS, La 3d, Zr 3d, and O 1s Nearly identical spectra were obtained for polycrystalline and single-crystal LLZTO with and without surface scratches
AFM, scratched single-crystal LLZTO Root mean square surface roughness Rq was approximately 28 nm; scratching increased the median flaw height by approximately one order of magnitude, from about 10 nm to 100 nm
Spherical nanoindentation, well-polished surfaces Both polycrystalline and single-crystal LLZTO initially exhibited Hertzian contact behavior; a sudden displacement burst and load drop were subsequently observed during further loading
Spherical nanoindentation, single-crystal LLZTO, 20 mN Pronounced indentation-induced cracks were observed
