Differential ECD, 1:1 (v/v) water/methanol, 25 °C On adding rac-[Ir(nBuBQ)₂Cl], the band around 460 nm changed its intensity in the positive or negative direction in the presence of Δ- or Λ-[Ru(phen)₃]²⁺, respectively.
