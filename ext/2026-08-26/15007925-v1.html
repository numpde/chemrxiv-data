CuFeS₂ nanocrystals
TEM and DLS Faceted nanocrystals with an average particle size of approximately 10 nm
Powder XRD The phase-pure tetragonal chalcopyrite CuFeS₂ phase was observed
UV–visible–NIR absorption, hexane A prominent optical resonance band was centered at 500 nm
EDX Cu, Fe, and S were present in ratios consistent with the expected CuFeS₂ composition; no peaks from oxidized phases or impurities were detected
Raman spectroscopy, dried on Si Chalcopyrite lattice vibrations were observed at 250–350 cm⁻¹; no impurity of different phases or defects was observed

1,2-bis(4-pyridyl)ethylene adsorbed on CuFeS₂ nanocrystals
SERS, 1 µM BPE, 488, 532, 633, and 785 nm excitation The excitation wavelengths yielded structurally identical vibrational profiles. No discernible fluctuations in peak positions were observed during time-resolved monitoring
Raman and SERS, 488 nm excitation The pristine substrate had no background Raman or photoluminescence signal from 800 to 1800 cm⁻¹; the dominant BPE band was the C=C ethylene stretching mode at 1640 cm⁻¹
Main, p. 7
p-nitrothiophenol adsorbed on CuFeS₂ nanocrystals
SERS, 457–785 nm excitation At 488 and 532 nm, the NTP band at 1335 cm⁻¹ was followed by the DMAB band at 1442 cm⁻¹ and subsequent thermal desorption of DMAB
Time-dependent SERS, 457 nm excitation DMAB bands near 1140, 1390, and 1442 cm⁻¹ appeared and progressively intensified with illumination time; the NTP NO₂ stretching mode at 1335 cm⁻¹ and the DMAB ag mode at 1442 cm⁻¹ were used for time tracking
SERS, irradiation longer than 500 s No evidence of further reduction of DMAB to p-aminothiophenol was observed
SERS, 488 nm excitation, 1 mW The characteristic DMAB modes decreased while broad bands centered at approximately 1350 and 1580 cm⁻¹ emerged; the bands were assigned to the D and G bands of amorphous carbon
Raman spectroscopy after dark heating, up to 145 °C for 5 min, 633 nm excitation, 7.6 kW cm⁻², 10 s acquisition No characteristic DMAB peaks were observed
