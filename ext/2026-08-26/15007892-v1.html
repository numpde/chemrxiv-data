Ni₄ and Ni₄/CNT
Cyclic voltammetry, Ni₄, DMF, TBA-BF₄ A small broad redox feature at −0.8 V vs Fc⁺/⁰ and a sharp wave at −1.7 V vs Fc⁺/⁰ were observed
UV–visible spectroscopy, Ni₄/CNT after OER A distinct blue shift from 407 nm to 393 nm was observed
UV–visible spectroscopy, Ni₄/CNT after HER A weak and poorly resolved band at roughly the same wavelength was detected
FESEM Ni₄/CNT showed irregular, nanoscale spots and aggregates of the Ni₄-complex-rich phase; after HER and OER electrolysis, the spots evolved into larger features with similar morphology
HRTEM, post-OER Lattice fringes with an interplanar spacing of ~0.28 nm were assigned to layered Ni(OH)₂/NiOOH-type phases
HRTEM, post-HER Lattice fringes with an interplanar spacing of ~0.28 nm were assigned to possible NiO/Ni(OH)₂-type domains, consistent with NiO (111) and Ni(OH)₂ (101) planes
XPS, Ni 2p₃/₂, post-OER The peak shifted from 853.8 to 855.6 eV, indicating surface oxidation and the likely formation of Ni(OH)₂/NiOOH-like species
XPS, Ni 2p₃/₂, post-HER A peak persisted near 855.8 eV, suggesting retained NiO/Ni(OH)₂-type domains rather than complete reduction to metallic Ni
