SERS at 785 nm in 0.1 M KNO₃ At acquisition times below 0.2 s per spectrum, spectral bands of distinct species appeared in only one or a few sequential spectra; averaging acquisitions to simulate exposure times above 0.5 s caused these bands to become part of the overall background intensity; with 50 ms exposure, 44 of 50 spectra showed only background and 6 showed contaminants
SERS during cyclic voltammetry in 0.1 M KNO₃ AuO formation was manifested by a broad Raman band at 570 cm⁻¹; the band area was integrated from 460 to 640 cm⁻¹, and the background was tracked from 700 to 720 cm⁻¹; the background intensity correlated strongly with appearance and disappearance of the AuO band, while repeatable background variation was also observed below the potential required for AuO formation
SERS during cyclic voltammetry in methanol The 570 cm⁻¹ AuO band was not observed over the 0.0 to 1.7 V versus Ag/AgCl potential window, while background intensity remained dependent on electrode potential
