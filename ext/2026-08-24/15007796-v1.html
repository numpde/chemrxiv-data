PXRD amorphous fraction and ICP-OES-derived protein fraction For TD–H₂O samples, the PXRD-derived amorphous fraction was systematically higher than the protein fraction, assigned to both protein and amorphous ZIF. For TD–EtOH samples with protein fraction > 20 %, the two measurements agreed, assigned primarily to protein.
