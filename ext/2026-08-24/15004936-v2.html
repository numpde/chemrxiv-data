Na₂.₅Fe₁.₇₅(SO₄)₃
XRD after 240 h moisture exposure Hydration was identified by the appearance of FeSO₄·H₂O diffraction peaks; hydration onset occurred at ~35 % RH (0.82 kPa) at 20 °C, ~36 % RH (1.14 kPa) at 25 °C, and ~38 % RH (2.13 kPa) at 35 °C
Gravimetry at the deliquescence boundary At ~95 % RH, rapid mass uptake and formation of a liquid phase were observed; the boundary remained effectively constant between 20 and 35 °C
Gravimetry and time-resolved XRD, 30 % RH, 25 °C Minimal mass change (~0.44 wt.%) and no detectable phase evolution over 240 h; no new diffraction peaks and unchanged phase composition within experimental uncertainty
Gravimetry and time-resolved XRD, 45 % RH, 25 °C 0–6 h: mass gain ~0.79 wt.% with no detectable phase change; from 12 h: diffraction peaks related to Na₂Fe(SO₄)₂·2H₂O and FeSO₄·H₂O emerged, with a minor increase in Na₆Fe(SO₄)₄; at 144 h: mass gain plateaued at ~7.6 wt.% and the alluaudite phase fraction reached ~37.94 % through 240 h
Time-resolved XRD, 45 % RH, 25 °C Hydrated phases emerged as sharp crystallographic transitions at 16.5–19° and 25.6–27.8° 2θ
Gravimetry and time-resolved XRD, 60 % RH, 25 °C ~3.7 % of the parent phase remained after 96–240 h; Na₆Fe(SO₄)₄ initially increased and disappeared by 48 h; Na₂Fe(SO₄)₂·2H₂O, Na₂Fe(SO₄)₂·4H₂O, FeSO₄·H₂O, and FeSO₄·7H₂O evolved sequentially, with higher hydrates increasingly prominent at later stages
Gravimetry after 240 h at 25 °C Mass gain reached ~0.44, 8.4, and 27.3 wt.% at 30, 45, and 60 % RH, respectively
Raman microscopy after 12 h at 25 °C 30 % RH: spectra remained unchanged; 45 and 60 % RH: SO₄²⁻ vibrational modes split at 990 cm⁻¹ and 1034 cm⁻¹; ≥80 % RH: sulfate Raman bands broadened; ~95 % RH: features were consistent with aqueous Na–Fe–SO₄ species, including Na/Fe–SO₄ contact ion-pair formation at 1080–1200 cm⁻¹, [Fe(H₂O)₆]²⁺ coordination at 370–410 cm⁻¹, and the H₂O bending mode at 1600 cm⁻¹
Macroscopic morphology after 12 h at 25 °C 30 % RH: unchanged; 45 and 60 % RH: progressive colour change from grey to light brown and particle aggregation; ≥80 % RH: significant particle agglomeration and loss of powder flowability; ~95 % RH: rapid moisture uptake (~49 wt.%) produced a viscous slurry
