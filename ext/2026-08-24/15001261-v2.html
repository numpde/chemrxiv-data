Sr3d XPS, Al Kα, Eₖ ~1350 eV Single doublet representing the bulk SrTiO₃ environment; fixed spin-orbit splitting 1.75 eV; bulk FWHM approximately 0.80 eV
Surface-sensitive Sr3d XPS, Eₖ = 600 eV and 150 eV Two additional surface components shifted by 0.6 eV and 1.1 eV toward higher binding energies relative to the bulk peak; both were significantly enhanced at Eₖ = 150 eV. The +1.1 eV peak was assigned to the reconstructed SrO overlayer and the +0.6 eV component to other surface oxide and hydroxylated species (SrOHₓ). Upon water exposure, the +0.6 eV feature intensity increased at the expense of the bulk and +1.1 eV SrO components
Surface-sensitive O1s XPS, Eₖ ~150 eV Low-doped samples: hydroxyl contribution around 12 at.% (± 1 %); 0.7 wt.% Nb-doped samples: higher hydroxyl contribution, with 21 at.% (± 1 %) for the reduced sample
