1 % w/v 900 kDa HA hydrogels with MgCl₂
Capillary-breakup extensional rheology, 7.5 mm plate separation Filament thinning was arrested at every MgCl₂ concentration across the 8.5 s observation window after a small initial decay; a non-crosslinked HA solution at the same polymer concentration showed exponential decay and broke within 2.5 s; arrest diameter increased with MgCl₂ concentration up to 60 mM and then showed no further change
Capillary-breakup extensional rheology, 10 mm plate separation The 20 mM MgCl₂ hydrogel produced an S-shaped curve and broke in 7.8 s; 30 and 50 mM formulations remained arrested over 30 s; 60 mM showed slight initial detachment followed by a plateau; 80 and 100 mM showed rapid complete detachment from the top plate

Capillary-breakup extensional rheology, molecular-weight series For 50 kDa HA, MgCl₂ and NaOH, separately or together, left breakup time and apparent extensional relaxation time largely unchanged, with all four formulations breaking within 40 ms; for 100 kDa HA, MgCl₂ increased and NaOH decreased breakup and relaxation times; for 250 and 500 kDa HA, MgCl₂ or NaOH alone decreased both values relative to HA alone; adding MgCl₂ and NaOH together produced the highest breakup and relaxation times at each molecular weight, with 100 and 250 kDa filaments breaking and 500 kDa filament thinning arrested over 8.5 s
