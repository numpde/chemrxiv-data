Adamantane and nitrogen-substituted adamantane HPHT products at 13 GPa
XRD and Raman at 1200 °C Adamantane produced cubic-diamond (111), (220), and (311) reflections, a first-order Raman line near 1331 cm⁻¹, a second-order feature near 2445 cm⁻¹, and C–H stretching bands; nitrogen-substituted precursors produced graphite-dominated products with graphite (002) diffraction and Raman D and G bands, while 2-AmAd also showed a weak diamond (111) reflection
XRD at 1370 and 1500 °C At 1370 °C, graphite reflections disappeared for 2-AzaAd, 2-AmAd, 1-CNAd, and 1-N₃Ad, while 1-AmAd remained predominantly graphitic; at 1500 °C, every substituted precursor produced a diamond-dominated product
ATR-FTIR Products from substituted precursors retained hydrogen-rich surface terminations and showed nitrogen-related features assigned to P1 and A centres

Adamantane-derivative pyrolysates at 450 °C
Raman and GC-MS Precursor preservation followed 1-N₃Ad < 1-CNAd, 2-AzaAd < 2-AmAd < 1-AmAd < adamantane; adamantane showed essentially no detectable decomposition; 1-AmAd and 2-AmAd retained pronounced sp³ C–H bands from 2900 to 3000 cm⁻¹, residual precursor, deaminated adamantane, and slightly higher-mass oligomers; 2-AzaAd and 1-CNAd showed prominent Raman D and G bands; 1-N₃Ad showed no residual precursor, while adamantane and 1-AmAd were detected among its products

2-AmAd at approximately 13 GPa
Time-resolved ex situ XRD at 1370 °C After 1 min, graphite was observed without detectable diamond reflections; after 5 min, a weak diamond (111) reflection appeared with graphite; after 15 min, graphite reflections disappeared and diamond became the dominant phase; longer heating sharpened the diamond (111) peak
In situ synchrotron XRD The crystalline precursor signal collapsed near 940 °C while a broad feature appeared at a d-spacing of approximately 2.9 Å; the feature persisted without diamond reflections to approximately 1300 °C and shifted to approximately 3.4 Å after quenching and decompression; the recovered product showed Raman D and G bands

Nitrogen-substituted-precursor nanodiamonds
Photoluminescence Broad emission features included weak signals near the expected NV⁰ and NV⁻ zero-phonon-line positions in some samples
X-band EPR at 1370 °C P1 concentrations were 398.4 ppm for 2-AzaAd, 256.9 ppm for 1-N₃Ad, 53.4 ppm for 1-CNAd, and 48.3 ppm for 2-AmAd; no clear NV⁻ signal was observed in any as-synthesized ensemble
ODMR, 2-AmAd-1500 Scarce NV-related emission and broad ODMR features were detected in isolated nanodiamonds; vacuum annealing at 800 °C did not produce a pronounced NV signal
EPR and ODMR after electron irradiation at 720 °C, 2-AmAd-1500 Half-field EPR showed an NV⁻ signal with an estimated concentration of 0.8 ± 0.15 ppm; spin relaxation time T1 was 2.85 ms and Hahn-echo coherence time T2 was 0.74 µs
