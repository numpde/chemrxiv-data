SPH- and STP-crosslinked ChNF hydrogels
Water content Above 97 %
EDX mapping Crosslinkers were homogeneously distributed throughout the hydrogel matrices
SEM Well-aligned pore channels were observed in transverse and longitudinal directions; freezing at −20 °C produced pore sizes of several hundred micrometers, while freezing at −196 °C produced pore sizes in the tens of micrometers; pore-wall orientation was predominantly axial at approximately 90°, with strong uniaxial ChNF alignment within the pore walls
WAXS Azimuthal integration of the (110) diffraction rings showed distinct intensity peaks at 90° and 270°
WAXS-derived orientation factor Orientation factors exceeded 0.5 and increased with ChNF content up to 1.2 wt. %; peak values were 0.8 for SPH-Ch1.2 % and 0.75 for STP-Ch1.2 %; orientation decreased above 1.2 wt. %

SPH-Ch1.2 % and STP-Ch1.2 % hydrogels
Uniaxial compression Both hydrogels showed nonlinear J-shaped stress–strain responses in transverse and longitudinal directions; at 20 % strain, longitudinal-direction modulus was 59.48 % lower than the transverse direction for SPH and 3.49 % lower for STP; at 95 % strain, longitudinal-direction modulus exceeded the transverse direction by 11.13 % for SPH and 25.35 % for STP
