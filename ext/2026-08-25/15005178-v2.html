Li₂FeCl₄
PXRD, milled sample Poor crystallinity made Rietveld analysis infeasible; the approximate 1:2 ratio of the peaks at 14° and 29° 2θ indicated formation of the Fd3̅m structure
PXRD, annealed samples Annealing at 300 °C retained the Fd3̅m structure; after 6 months at room temperature, 27 % of the Fd3̅m phase had converted to Cmmm, increasing to 32 % after 9 months; the product annealed at 400 °C contained 14 % Cmmm and 86 % Fd3̅m
EIS at 25 °C Ionic conductivity was 0.023 mS cm⁻¹ for Fd3̅m Li₂FeCl₄ and decreased to 0.0038 and 0.0014 mS cm⁻¹ in samples containing 27 and 32 % Cmmm, respectively
Main, pp. 10–12, 14–15
Li₂Fe₁−ₓMnₓCl₄
PXRD All compositions, synthesized in steps of x = 0.25, formed the Fd3̅m polymorph and contained approximately 4 wt % LiCl; the refined lattice parameter increased linearly with x, and both the metal octahedra and Li⁺ tetrahedra increased in volume with increasing Mn²⁺ content; none of the Mn²⁺-containing samples converted to Cmmm or Imma after 6 months
SEM-EDX Measured Fe:Mn ratios from multiple particles closely matched the nominal compositions
EIS, 25–60 °C A single semicircle was observed for every sample at every temperature; at 25 °C, ionic conductivity decreased from 0.023 mS cm⁻¹ for Li₂FeCl₄ to 0.0012 mS cm⁻¹ for Li₂MnCl₄; activation energy increased from 0.46 to 0.57 eV across the same end members
DC polarization at 25 °C Electronic conductivities were approximately 10⁻¹⁰ S cm⁻¹, about five orders of magnitude below the ionic conductivities, with no distinct compositional trend
