Iodide chemical-ionization mass spectrometry Transient peaks with formulas consistent with trifluoroacetic acid, C₂HF₃O₂; perfluoropropanoic acid, C₃HF₅O₂; and 3,3,3-trifluoropropan-1-ol, C₃H₅F₃O, were observed and subsided to background levels within 30 min; the peaks were not present in later experiments

Bleach-wiping and HOCl-fogging experiments
Cl₂ and HOCl concentration measurements Concentrations increased to tens to hundreds of ppb before declining
Background Cl₂ concentration measurements Background concentrations were highest on the first day after bleach or HOCl application and decreased over subsequent days without another application; after five or more days, average background concentrations were close to outdoor levels
Background HOCl concentration measurements Concentrations were more variable and showed no clear relationship with the number of days since bleach wiping or HOCl fogging
