Eu/Y dilution series
PXRD The diffraction profiles were globally preserved across the series
Raman spectroscopy, 633 nm excitation The main Raman features were preserved across the dilution series; sample-dependent relative-intensity changes were observed, including in the approximately 1650 cm⁻¹ region
Low-frequency Raman spectroscopy, 633 nm excitation The main spectral features remained broadly similar, while sample-to-sample relative-intensity changes were observed; at approximately 15 K, the main room-temperature bands of the 100 % Eu and 100 % Eu–D samples generally split into patterns of sharp peaks with linewidths comparable to the approximately 2 cm⁻¹ spectral resolution
Photoluminescence, approximately 10 K The same overall emission profile was observed across the concentration range; emission was dominated by the ⁵D₀→⁷F₂ transition around 612 nm
