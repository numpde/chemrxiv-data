Characteristic surface-enhanced Raman bands: NTP (e.g.); DMAB NTP NO₂ stretching vibration at 1335 cm⁻¹; under near resonant excitation conditions, distinct DMAB azobenzene stretching vibrations appeared near 1140, 1390, and 1442 cm⁻¹, including the a₍g₎ modes of DMAB at 1442 cm⁻¹
Time-series surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy, 457 nm excitation The DMAB bands appeared in addition to the NTP signals and progressively intensified with illumination time; temporal evolution of normalized SERS intensities for the reactant band at 1335 cm⁻¹ and product band at 1442 cm⁻¹ was extracted from the spectra
High laser power densities Carbonization and a distinct “burning” of the sample were observed; the characteristic DMAB vibrational modes decreased while two broad Raman bands centered at approximately 1350 and 1580 cm⁻¹ emerged, assigned to the D (disorder) and G (graphitic) bands of amorphous carbon, respectively
