UV–Vis absorption spectroscopy Three major bands were observed: 272 nm, corresponding to a π–π* transition involving aromatic C=C bonds in the carbon core; a tail at 349 nm, indicating intrinsic absorption of the n–π* transition of C=O in the carbon cores; and 540 nm, originating from surface-state transitions involving lone-pair electrons from C=O and C=N surface functional groups
