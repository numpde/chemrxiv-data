Calcite crystal during dissolution in aqueous solution
Time-lapse fluorescence imaging immediately above the crystal surface pH increased from approximately 6 and stabilized at approximately 9; Ca2+ concentration rapidly increased to approximately 200 µM
Time-lapse fluorescence imaging around the crystal pH and Ca2+ concentrations increased concentrically; Ca2+ concentration increased at crystal protrusions, whereas the pH distribution became more concentric
Dissolution rate derived from the imaged Ca2+ concentration profile using DCa = 1 × 10⁻⁵ cm² s⁻¹ approximately 10-10.0 mol cm⁻² s⁻¹ just after sinking into solution; approximately 10-10.8 mol cm⁻² s⁻¹ after 30 min
OH− amount after 30 min 4.9 × 10⁻¹⁵ mol calculated from the experimentally obtained Ca2+ concentration under the stated dissolution, equilibrium, and diffusion assumptions; approximately 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁵ mol calculated directly from the measured pH change using the ionic product of water
