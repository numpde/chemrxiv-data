LaCrO₃, LaFeO₃ and LaCoO₃ thin films
XPS (1.8 keV) and HAXPES (5.9 keV), survey spectra Characteristic peaks corresponding to La, O and the respective transition metals Cr, Fe and Co were observed. Sr and Ti features from the substrate were present in LaCrO₃ and LaFeO₃, but were not detected in LaCoO₃
XPS (1.8 keV) and HAXPES (5.9 keV), valence band Spectral weight near the Fermi level (0–2 eV) was attributed to transition-metal 3d states; broad structures between 2 and 9 eV were attributed mainly to oxygen 2p states
XPS and HAXPES, La 3d A satellite feature was observed in all compounds at about 4 eV higher binding energy than the main 3d₅/₂ and 3d₃/₂ peaks; the satellite had comparable spectral intensity to the main line
