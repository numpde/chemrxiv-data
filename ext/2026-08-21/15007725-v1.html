X-ray diffraction Strong (00l) reflections for exfoliated MoS₂ before exfoliation and CVT MoS₂; broader peaks and random orientations for HT MoS₂

High-resolution transmission electron microscopy (100) interplanar spacing 0.27–0.28 nm; uniform atomic arrangement, well-defined lattice fringes, and no observable defects for CVD MoS₂; uniform atomic arrangement for exfoliated MoS₂; uniform atomic arrangement with a few voids and atom dislocations for CVT MoS₂; smaller grain size with retained 2H hexagonal lattice for HT MoS₂

CVT MoS₂ heated under N₂
In situ variable-temperature Raman spectroscopy, standard-grade N₂ with 5 ppm O₂ Only 2H-MoS₂ peaks at approximately 380 and 408 cm⁻¹ below 600 °C; a new peak at approximately 200 cm⁻¹ appeared near 600 °C; MoS₂ peaks disappeared above 725 °C; new peaks from approximately 126 to 740 cm⁻¹ were assigned to monoclinic P2₁/c MoO₂; the spectrum remained unchanged after cooling and after 23 days in ambient conditions
Transmission electron microscopy after heat treatment and cooling Larger sheets and smaller rounded particles; 0.35 nm lattice-fringe spacing in rounded particles, assigned to the MoO₂ (011) plane; selected-area electron diffraction indexed larger sheets to MoS₂ and rounded particles to monoclinic MoO₂
Thermogravimetric analysis and variable-temperature Raman spectroscopy Transitions at approximately 92, 262, 481, 720, and 800 °C; 0.07 % mass loss below 92 °C with increased MoS₂ Raman intensity; 0.55 % mass loss and rapid Raman-intensity decrease from 92 to 262 °C; 0.2 % mass loss and gradual Raman-intensity decrease from 262 to 481 °C; loss of MoS₂ peaks and growth of MoO₂ peaks through 720 °C; weakened MoO₂ peaks and 0.06 % mass loss through 800 °C
In situ variable-temperature Raman spectroscopy, 660 °C MoS₂-, MoO₂-, and Mo₄O₁₁-predominant spectra after holding for 30 min; only MoO₃ spectra after 52 min
Raman spectroscopy of the heating-stage window S₈ peaks from 100 to 350 °C; blue coating produced under N₂ yielded MoO₃ peaks after exposure to air; S₈ also appeared transiently under H₂/N₂, without a blue coating
