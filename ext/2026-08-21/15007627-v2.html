P2, P6, and P8 azido-peptides with C6, C8, and C12 alkynes
High-performance liquid chromatography after CuAAC initiation Each combination showed an immediate decrease in the azido-peptide peak within the first 5 min and a new peak at higher retention time; monoexponential starting-material consumption; equilibrium conversion 35–60 %; all combinations reached equilibrium within approximately 20 min
DiO-C6 fluorescence after CuAAC initiation C6- and C8-derived peptide amphiphiles assembled rapidly relative to C12-derived peptide amphiphiles; peptide-precursor alkyl-spacer length had negligible influence on assembly rates
Circular dichroism Almost all derivatives showed a random-coil signature; P8C12 showed β-sheet character
Confocal fluorescence microscopy after 5 h C6- and C8-based peptide amphiphiles showed isotropic colloidal morphology of micrometer-sized particles; P6C12 and P8C12 formed well-defined fibers with hierarchical bundling; P2C12 showed irregular architectures
Confocal fluorescence microscopy after 24 h P2C12 transitioned into fiber bundles matching those of P6C12 and P8C12; fibrous morphology remained inaccessible to P2C6 and P2C8 after prolonged aging
