Soluble V⁵⁺ with glycerol and glycerol-oxidation products
UV–Vis absorption spectroscopy, Na₂CO₃ and Na₂SO₄ electrolytes Glycerol, formate, and glycolate showed negligible V⁵⁺ reduction in either electrolyte; glyceraldehyde, dihydroxyacetone, and glycolaldehyde readily reduced V⁵⁺ in carbonate with comparable kinetics; in sulfate, glyceraldehyde and dihydroxyacetone reduced V⁵⁺ at significantly slower rates and glycolaldehyde was essentially unreactive
