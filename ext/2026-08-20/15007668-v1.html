Main, pp. 14–15
D- and L-tartaric acid on 169 Cu surface orientations vicinal to Cu(100)
O 1s spatially resolved X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy, 433 K Coverage decay curves from all 169 orientations showed initiation and explosion features for both enantiomers. The coverage maps showed four-fold rotational symmetry. At 31 % extent of reaction, decomposition began in regions with high step-edge density; D- and L-tartaric-acid decomposition began in the R- and S-chirality regions, respectively, producing mirror symmetry between the enantiomers. Maps from 31 to 70 % extent of reaction showed diastereomerism. From 75 to 85 % extent of reaction, Cu(100) was more reactive than some highly stepped and kinked orientations
O 1s spatially resolved X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy, rate-law fits The initiation and explosion rate constants showed no clear dependence on surface orientation and no notable symmetry; their dependence on surface orientation was below the detection limit
