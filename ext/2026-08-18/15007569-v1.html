Alumina containing 15 wt.% dysprosium

Alumina containing 20 wt.% dysprosium
SEM Large faceted particles, dense agglomerates, and well-defined geometric surfaces. Large polygonal and block-like particles possess smooth crystallographic faces and sharp edges. The particles appear tightly packed and interconnected, forming compact agglomerates with minimal visible void spaces between adjacent grains

Alumina containing 25 wt.% dysprosium
SEM A highly crystalline morphology characterized by discrete faceted particles, well-defined geometric structures, and a relatively uniform particle size distribution. The particles exhibit predominantly polyhedral and angular morphologies with clearly developed crystallographic faces. Large block-like crystals with smooth surfaces and sharp edges dominate the micrograph
