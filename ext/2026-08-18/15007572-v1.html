Bismuth electrodes in chloride and sulfate electrolytes
Cyclic voltammetry, 2 mV s⁻¹, 0.50 M NaCl A single dominant oxidation peak at approximately 0.18 V, followed by a major reduction peak at approximately −1.04 V
Cyclic voltammetry, 2 mV s⁻¹, 0.33 M Na2SO4 A broad oxidation response with two small peaks at approximately −0.02 V and 0.24 V, followed by reduction peaks at approximately −0.57 and −0.85 V
Cyclic voltammetry, 2 mV s⁻¹, 0.25 and 0.50 M NaCl The oxidation peak shifted from approximately 0.25 to 0.15 V, and the reduction peak shifted from approximately −1.27 to −1.17 V
