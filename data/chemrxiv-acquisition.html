ChemRxiv acquisition method

This procedure collects ChemRxiv papers with plausible experimental characterization, together with their available Supporting Information.

  1. Build the daily manifest.

    Query Crossref for every ChemRxiv posted-content record on one exact date. Write a manifest.json that records the query, retrieval time, source, and DOIs.

  2. Screen the submissions.

    Review titles and abstracts manually. Retain papers plausibly containing experimental characterization, including NMR, Raman, IR, MS, diffraction, electrochemistry, photophysics, synthesis characterization, and similar data. Exclude purely computational, machine-learning, software, conceptual, and perspective papers.

  3. Download one submission at a time.

    Use the existing local Chromium session to open the article page and read its main PDF and Supporting Information links. Download one file at a time and move validated files into data/YYYY-MM-DD/ before proceeding to the next submission.

  4. Preserve the useful files.

    • Main PDFs.
    • Documentary Supporting Information in PDF, DOCX, or XLSX format.
    • Publisher-provided Supporting Information archives in ZIP format.
    • Non-documentary data such as XYZ and CIF files, consolidated into a *-si-additional.zip archive.
    • A *-si.txt note when no separate Supporting Information is available or when the only available supplement is an omitted video.

  5. Keep downloaded content inert.

    Omit videos. Never execute downloaded code, macros, scripts, installers, or other active content.

  6. Validate the completed date.

    • Check PDF signatures, readability, and main-paper titles.
    • Check ZIP, DOCX, and XLSX container integrity.
    • Confirm that every retained DOI occurs in the manifest.
    • Confirm that every main paper has either Supporting Information or an explicit no-SI note.
    • Confirm that no partial downloads or temporary files remain.
    • Close the acquisition tabs, leaving only example.com open in Chromium.

  7. Respect access limits.

    Stop immediately if ChemRxiv returns a challenge or rate-limit block. Never parallelize downloads or hammer retries.

  8. Leave publication explicit.

    Do not commit the results unless a commit is explicitly requested.
